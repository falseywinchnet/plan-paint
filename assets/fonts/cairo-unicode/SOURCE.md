# Cairo Unicode

Source: https://fontlibrary.org/en/font/cairo-unicode
Download: https://fontlibrary.org/assets/downloads/cairo-unicode/f7a36c1d8f12b46ccdd6dd3726c8cdfe/cairo-unicode.zip

The unmodified Cairo.ttf and its three original notices accompany the free Paint distribution. Clark T. Riley authored the outline face; ChristTrekker added Unicode mappings. This is the dingbat face, not the unrelated Cairo Arabic/Latin family.

The font is freeware, not MIT licensed. The original notices permit personal distribution with the notices and require the author's written permission for commercial distribution. Keep all three notices with the font; Paint's code license does not change these terms.
