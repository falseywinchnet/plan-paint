CAIRO TRUETYPE

This is a TrueType version of the popular freeware font Cairo, which was designed by Clark Riley and uploeaded to America Online as a PostScript Type 1 typeface. I used Ares FontMonger 1.5.7 to convert Cairo to TrueType format; otherwise, I have made no changes to the font.

Riley has given permission for this conversion by the following e-mail message:

Date:  Jul 7, 1994 08:54 EST
From:  DrRiley
Subj:  Re: A TrueType Cairo?
To:      RussMSEN

You will get almost instantaneous response if you use my InterNet address (can be used within America Online) - clark.riley@qmail.bs.jhu.edu.
