# Plan Paint 1.1.2

Plan Paint 1.1.2 fixes body protection for guides created from a selection.

## Filled selection guides

A tightening lasso around an outlined shape selects its ink. Previously, turning that selection into a guide left its enclosed center paintable even when Fill was enabled. Switching to Brush hid the editing handles, making the narrow remaining stencil look as though it had disappeared.

With Fill enabled, selection-derived guides now protect the enclosed body as well as the selected ink. The protected area remains visibly hatched when switching to Brush. Both Brush and Guide stay highlighted: Brush is the current tool and Guide is the active stencil. Turning Fill off restores boundary-only protection.

The conversion preserves feathered exterior edges and leaves exterior-connected gaps open. It prepares the body mask once, keeping per-pixel painting checks constant-time. The original artwork is unchanged by creating the guide.

## Compatibility

This patch retains the Windows language fixes, package optimizations, settings and file compatibility of 1.1.1. The Forms interface remains the released frontend. macOS requires version 26 or newer; Linux requires X11 or XWayland.
